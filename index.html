Your EFB needs a position. Most simulators won't give it one.

Full-flight and training simulators rarely expose their position in a form an iPad EFB can read, and wiring into one usually isn't an option. Sim Beacon reads the position straight off the operator's screen with your iPhone camera and streams it to the EFB — so it works even on closed simulators with no data output of their own.

How it works

Three steps, nothing wired in.

01 · Aim

Point the camera

Mount your iPhone so its camera sees the simulator's latitude/longitude readout. No screen to read? Use Map mode — trace a route and fly it.

02 · Read

Read the position

Sim Beacon recognises the coordinates on screen in any common notation, and tracks them smoothly as the aircraft moves.

03 · Transmit

Send it to the EFB

The position streams over Wi-Fi to the iPad, and own-ship moves on the chart in real time — with track and ground speed.
